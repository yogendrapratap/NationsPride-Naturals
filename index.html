Questions people ask first

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app, name your collection centre and start. There is nothing to sign up for.

Where is my data stored?

In a SQLite database inside this browser, on the phone or computer you are using. It is not sent to a server, and other devices do not see it. Clearing your browser data would remove it, so download a backup from the Reports page regularly. To move to another phone or computer, restore that backup file there.

How do I find a farmer or an entry?

Open Search and type a farmer's name, code, village or mobile number. You can also type a date like 12 sep, a shift, an animal, a payment mode or an amount. A farmer code such as 7 or 007 jumps straight to that farmer.

Does it connect to a milk analyzer?

No. You read fat and SNF from your analyzer and type them in. The rate is then worked out from your chart.

Can cow and buffalo milk have different rates?

Yes. Each animal has its own rate per fat point and per SNF point, and you can add an evening adjustment in rupees per litre.

What happens to old entries if I change the rate?

Nothing. Each entry stores the rate it was saved with, so past payments stay correct. Only new entries use the new rate.

How do farmers get their details?

Every entry has an SMS link. It opens your phone's messaging app with the litres, fat, SNF, rate and amount already written; you press send. Messages are not sent automatically.

Can I get my records into Excel?

Yes. On the Reports page, download the entries or the farmer-wise summary as a CSV file, which opens in Excel and Google Sheets.
